Gravity Playground

A pocket universe of bouncy balls. Spawn them, throw them, flip gravity to zero and watch beautiful chaos happen.

Tap anywhere to drop a ball · drag a ball to throw it

0 balls in orbit
How to play
  • Tap empty space to drop a ball where your finger is.
  • Drag any ball to pick it up — release while moving to throw it.
  • Sliders tune the universe: gravity from zero to double-Earth, wall bounciness, and the size (mass) of freshly spawned balls.
  • Balls bounce off each other and the walls, heavier ones push lighter ones around.
  • The pause button freezes time; the trash button starts a new universe. Fullscreen mode is great on phones.
