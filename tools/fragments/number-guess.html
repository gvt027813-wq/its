How to play
  • Choose a range, then guess. The lab answers higher or lower.
  • The shaded bar shows the live range where the number can still hide.
  • Par is the perfect score: ceil(log2(range)) guesses. Hit par or better for a perfect badge.
  • Duplicate guesses are gently mocked but not counted twice.
  • Your best (fewest guesses) per range is saved on this device.
