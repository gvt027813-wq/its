Word Mixer

You own four humble things. Mix any two to brew a new one, then mix again — a whole dictionary of discoveries is hiding in your drawer.

How to play
  • Tap two things in your drawer (or tap the slots to pick your latest finds).
  • Press Mix it. Some pairs brew something brand new; many don't.
  • First discoveries are marked with a spark and a small note about the pair that got you there.
  • Everything you brew is saved on this device, forever (or until you hit reset).
  • There is no final word — recipes run deep and the kettle never quite runs dry.
