How to play
  • Study the big target swatch, then tap the one option that matches it exactly.
  • Easy gives bold decoys, Hard gives near-identical evil twins.
  • Correct picks score 10 (Easy), 15 (Medium) or 25 (Hard) points, multiplied by your current streak.
  • A wrong pick resets the streak and reveals the true match — then the next round begins.
  • After ten rounds you get a lab report; the best score per difficulty is saved on this device.
