About FunLab

FunLab is a small collection of original, playful web experiments — the kind of things you open for thirty seconds and stay for because “one more try”.

Why does FunLab exist?

Because the web is at its best when it is silly. Every experiment here is an original little machine built from scratch with plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step you need to run, no accounts — just toys that load fast and run anywhere, including a budget Android phone on flaky Wi‑Fi.

What's inside the lab?

Ten experiments across six flavours:

  • Arcade — Click Rush and Reaction Lab, for competitive fingers.
  • Physics — Gravity Playground, a sandbox of bouncing balls you can throw around.
  • Puzzle — Color Master and Number Guess, for careful eyes and clever halves.
  • Creative — Tiny Drawing and Word Mixer, where discoveries stack up.
  • Random — Decision Machine and Random Planets, for when the universe should choose.
  • Learn — Password Lab, which shows why some passwords crumble instantly.

How it's built

The whole platform is dependency-free: semantic HTML, modern CSS and ES2022 JavaScript split into small reusable components — cards, navigation, modals, toasts, theming, sound, scores, fullscreen and storage. Heavy experiments are loaded only on their own pages, canvases respect your device's pixel ratio, and everything honours the prefers-reduced-motion setting.

FunLab is also a Progressive Web App: install it from your browser menu and it keeps working offline thanks to a service worker that caches the lab on your device.

Your data is yours

High scores, saved drawings, discovered words and your theme choice live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. There is no server to send them to. The full story is on the Privacy page, which also has a one-tap “erase everything” button.
