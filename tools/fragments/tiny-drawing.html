Tiny Drawing

A calm little canvas with a silky brush, a forgiving eraser and one-tap PNG export. Doodle something unwise.

Strokes autosave to this browser — come back later and your doodle will wait patiently. Saving a PNG downloads it to your device.

How to play
  • Pick a colour swatch (or the custom colour well), then paint with your finger, stylus or mouse.
  • The size slider changes the brush; the eraser paints in canvas colour for clean corrections.
  • Undo steps back through your recent strokes; Clear wipes the canvas after a confirmation.
  • PNG downloads your artwork. A copy also autosaves quietly in this browser.
  • Drawing works in portrait and landscape — rotate freely, the canvas keeps its 4:3 shape.
