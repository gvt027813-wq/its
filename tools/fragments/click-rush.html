How to play
  • Tap the big button once to start the 10-second timer.
  • Then tap, tap, tap as fast as humanly (or inhumanly) possible.
  • On a touchscreen you may use several fingers at once — the machine counts every one.
  • When the timer ends you get your CPS score. Your personal best is saved on this device only.
