How to play
  • Type an option and press Add — repeat until every choice is on the table (up to 12).
  • No ideas? Surprise options loads a silly starter set.
  • Pull the lever and watch the drum hammer through your options with increasing drama.
  • The winner is picked with cryptographically fair randomness and celebrated with confetti.
  • Recent verdicts are remembered on this device so you can admire the machine's wisdom.
