What am I looking at?
  • Every planet is generated on your device from a random seed: no two visits paint the same sky.
  • The portrait is drawn live on a canvas — colours, bands, craters, rings and moons all roll from the seed.
  • Name, climate, terrain, population and civilisation come from the same seed, so a saved planet can be repainted exactly.
  • Tap the star to save a world to your collection (stored only on this device); tap a saved chip to revisit it.
  • Copy writes a catalogue entry to your clipboard; Share uses your device's share sheet where available.
