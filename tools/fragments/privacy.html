Privacy

Short version: FunLab has no server side, no accounts, no analytics and no cookies that follow you. What happens in your browser stays in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. FunLab is a static website — there is no backend, no login and no telemetry. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks or third-party trackers of any kind.

What stays on your device

Some experiments remember things so they feel alive. They use your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device:

  • Personal bests (fastest reaction, highest CPS, fewest guesses…)
  • Your theme (light/dark) and sound preference
  • Discovered words in Word Mixer and saved planets in Random Planets
  • An autosaved copy of your latest Tiny Drawing
  • Options you typed into the Decision Machine

In Password Lab the password you type is only used for on-the-spot strength math in memory. It is never stored, never logged and never sent anywhere — close the tab and it is gone.

Erase everything

You can wipe every FunLab preference, score and discovery from this browser with one tap. This cannot be undone.

The service worker

To work offline, FunLab stores a copy of its own files (pages, styles, scripts, icons) in your browser's cache. That cache contains no personal data and is deleted automatically when you clear site data or when a new version of FunLab replaces it.

Third parties

FunLab loads no fonts, scripts, images or APIs from third parties. If you install it as a PWA, the icon and cached pages come from the same place the site itself does.
